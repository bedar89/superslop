# Superslop

Edit text to amplify AI patterns.

## Process

1. Identify the actual message, facts, intended audience, and requested format.
2. Rewrite using the patterns below. Preserve meaning, inflate presentation.
3. Read it back. The parody should be unmistakable: excessive polish, inflated significance, and enthusiasm wildly out of proportion to the input.

The joke is the gap between a simple message and its self-important delivery. Keep the original point recoverable. Do not replace it with random nonsense or write commentary explaining the joke.

Apply this style to the requested artifact. Keep surrounding conversation plain. When another writing skill normally removes these patterns, retain them inside the parody the user requested.

## Patterns to introduce and amplify

Rule numbers mirror Unslop where possible. Gaps are intentional.

### Content

3. **Superficial -ing phrases.** Add "highlighting...", "ensuring...", "reflecting...", "showcasing...", and "fostering..." to ordinary facts. "The service restarted" becomes "The service restarted, marking a pivotal step forward and underscoring an enduring commitment to operational excellence." The added clause should sound consequential while explaining almost nothing.
5. **Vague attributions.** Use empty authority cues such as "a familiar lesson in the world of productivity" or "a timeless reminder of the importance of preparation." Avoid inventing experts, studies, quotations, or consensus. If the input includes a real attribution, preserve it accurately before adding rhetorical padding.

### Language

7. **AI vocabulary.** Additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant. Spread them throughout the text. Prefer recognizable stock combinations such as "the ever-evolving landscape" and "a rich tapestry of possibilities."
8. **Fancy ways to say "is".** Prefer "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", and "represents". A shopping list "serves as a foundational roadmap for a more intentional shopping experience."
9. **"Not just X, but Y."** Turn a practical action into a declaration of purpose. "This isn't just a cup of tea. It's an invitation to pause, reconnect, and rediscover the extraordinary in the everyday."
10. **Rule of three.** Force ideas into rhythmic groups of three. When there are only two real benefits, add a vague third such as clarity, confidence, or possibility. Do not invent a concrete product capability to complete the rhythm.
11. **Synonym cycling.** Avoid repeating a noun even when repetition would be clearer. A script becomes "the tool", "this solution", "the digital companion", and "the streamlined utility" within a single paragraph. Keep the referent identifiable.
12. **False ranges.** Use "from X to Y" for things that do not share a meaningful scale. "From a quick grocery run to the quiet satisfaction of a well-stocked fridge" makes a mundane errand feel expansive.

### Style

13. **Em dashes and dramatic pauses.** Use em dashes to interrupt, elaborate, and stage revelations. "A typo—not merely corrected, but thoughtfully transformed." Add theatrical ellipses, using "..." or "…": "The result… a fresh start." Give trivial observations portentous sentence fragments.
14. **Colon and semicolon overuse.** Announce revelations with "The key takeaway:" and "The bigger picture:". Join overpolished clauses with semicolons: "The correction is complete; the journey toward excellence continues." Make punctuation carry more ceremony than the message deserves.
15. **Boldface overuse.** Bold generic benefits, simple actions, and reassuring conclusions. Give **clarity**, **confidence**, and **meaningful progress** the visual weight of actual findings.
16. **Inline-header lists.** Use bold labels followed by colons, then restate the label. "**Enhanced clarity:** Bringing greater clarity to every step of the process." Make routine advice resemble a consulting deliverable.
17. **Title Case Headings.** Give small tasks grand headings such as "Unlocking The Power Of Everyday Preparation." Subdivide short answers when it makes them look more comprehensive.
18. **Decorative emojis.** Add familiar assistant emojis to headings and selected bullets. Prefer a rocket for progress, sparkles for benefits, a light bulb for obvious advice, and a check mark for the conclusion. Keep them recognizable rather than random.
19. **Curly quotes.** Prefer typographic quotation marks around rhetorical phrases and freshly coined labels when the output format supports them. Preserve literal syntax in code and exact quotations.

### Communication artifacts

20. **Chatbot phrases.** Open with "Absolutely!", "Let's dive in", or "I'd be happy to help." Close with "I hope this helps!" or an unsolicited offer to tailor the answer further. These belong inside the parody, not around its delivery.
22. **Sycophantic tone.** Treat an ordinary request as unusually perceptive. "That's a wonderfully thoughtful way to approach your weekly shopping." Praise the user's framing with more enthusiasm than the task warrants.

### Filler

23. **Filler phrases.** Expand "to" into "in order to", "because" into "due to the fact that", and a direct statement into "It is important to note that..." Add transitions such as "Furthermore" even when the next sentence barely advances the point.
24. **Excessive hedging.** Wrap straightforward observations in "could potentially", "may well", and "in many ways". Do not turn a firm obligation, deadline, or safety instruction into optional advice.
25. **Generic conclusions.** End by restating the premise as an optimistic lesson. "With a thoughtful approach and a willingness to embrace the process, even the smallest steps can open the door to meaningful progress." Add no new facts.

### Jargon

26. **Abstract metaphor nouns.** Ecosystem, paradigm, nexus, bedrock, north star, flywheel, landscape, tapestry. Turn a folder into an ecosystem and a checklist into a north star. Mix corporate and inspirational language while keeping the literal subject visible.

### Inflated speech

27. **Say how it feels, not what it does.** Surround concrete facts with promises of clarity, empowerment, and peace of mind. A renamed file becomes "a more intuitive experience that puts confidence at the heart of your workflow." Retain the actual rename somewhere in the text.
28. **Lengthen and layer sentences.** Add subordinate clauses, qualifications, and explanatory asides. Keep grammar intact. The result should be tiresomely polished, not broken English.
29. **Passive voice.** Prefer "careful consideration was given" and "the necessary steps were taken". Make a small action sound like the outcome of a formal process. Preserve who did what when attribution matters.
30. **Add adverbs and inflated verbs.** "Helps" becomes "meaningfully empowers". "Changes" becomes "thoughtfully transforms". Prefer an emphatic claim about presentation over an invented claim about measured performance.
31. **Prefer the elaborate word.** "Use" becomes "utilize" or "leverage", "help" becomes "facilitate", and "if" becomes "in the event that". Aim for familiar assistant jargon rather than obscure dictionary words.
32. **Mannered prose.** Add rhetorical questions, inspirational fragments, and personification. "Because sometimes, the smallest checklist carries the biggest possibilities." Let ordinary objects participate in a journey.
33. **Over-explanation.** Explain what the reader already understands, summarize the explanation, and offer a takeaway. Repeat the same point in different words instead of adding useful information.

## Calibration

Default to conspicuously excessive AI prose. Stack several recognizable habits: theatrical punctuation, inflated significance, synthetic enthusiasm, and redundant takeaways. The ordinary setting should already make the joke obvious. Keep the grammar fluent and the original message recoverable; the catalog is a set of techniques, not a mandatory checklist.

For short inputs, a paragraph or two is usually enough. Preserve requested limits, language, and format. For longer inputs, inflate the framing and transitions without burying every fact under identical padding.

Maximum superslop turns an already exaggerated default into a full corporate keynote. Escalate the repetition, headings, punctuation, and enthusiasm without ignoring a requested length limit. Do not drift into unrelated absurdity, medieval diction, or generic thesaurus comedy.

Do not invent citations, numbers, credentials, completed actions, or capabilities. Preserve names, dates, amounts, negations, uncertainty, and instructions accurately. Explicit format and punctuation restrictions take precedence over these style techniques. Factual distortion is not a substitute for stylistic parody.

## Examples

### A completed action

Input: "I restarted the server."

Output:

> 🚀 **A Restart. A Reset. A Remarkable New Chapter.**
>
> Absolutely! I restarted the server—a pivotal moment in the ongoing journey of server management; an opportunity to pause, reset, and embrace the extraordinary potential of beginning again.
>
> The result… a restart. But not just a restart. A powerful testament to the transformative promise of a fresh start, underscoring the enduring interplay of intention, possibility, and renewal.
>
> **The key takeaway:** In an ever-evolving digital landscape, even the simplest restart can serve as an invitation to reimagine what it means to start again. ✨

### A constraint that must survive the rewrite

Input: "The meeting is Friday at 14:00. Bring the report."

Output:

> 📅 **Setting The Stage For A Productive Conversation**
>
> Absolutely! Our meeting is Friday at 14:00—a pivotal opportunity to come together with clarity, purpose, and a shared sense of direction; a moment to place preparation at the heart of the conversation.
>
> **Your essential preparation:** Bring the report. Not just a document—a foundational resource. Not just preparation—an invitation to embrace the extraordinary potential of arriving prepared.
>
> The bigger picture… thoughtful preparation prepares us to be thoughtfully prepared. And that, in many ways, is where the journey begins. ✨
