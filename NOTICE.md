# Attribution

Superslop is an independent parody of the Unslop writing skill in
[pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack), by Lauren Tan.

The skill adapts Unslop's category structure, stable rule numbers, pattern names,
and selected vocabulary, reversing its editorial purpose. The process,
calibration guidance, and worked examples were written for Superslop.
